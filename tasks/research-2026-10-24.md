## 2. AI accounts, models, cost

- **Subscriptions:** Anthropic consumer terms prohibit automated access except via API key and making an account available to others ([terms](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/consumer-terms)). OpenAI terms prohibit programmatic extraction, credential sharing and circumventing rate limits *(quotes from summaries)*. Google AI Pro/Ultra apply only in the AI Studio web interface; API use is billed separately ([docs](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/google-ai-plans)).
- **Models ($ per million tokens, input/output):** Gemini 3.8 Flash $0.75/$3.75 (doubles 2027-01-01), Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite $0.25/$1.50, gpt-5.4-mini $0.75/$4.50, gpt-5-mini $0.25/$2.00, Claude Haiku 5.5 $0.10/$0.50, Claude Sonnet 5.5 $2/$10.
- **Latency:** Gemini 3.8 Flash at full thinking ~25 s to first token (Artificial Analysis); Claude Haiku 5.5 (high) ~24 s. Lower thinking should be much faster — not measured.
- **Demo cost estimate (500 questions + 50 brand profiles):** ~$1 (Haiku 5.5) to ~$21 (Sonnet 5.5).
- **Routing:** Vercel AI Gateway + AI SDK — no markup, ordered fallbacks, spend caps, bring-your-own keys.

## 4. Push channels

- **Slack:** incoming webhooks, ~1 hour of setup.
- **Email:** Resend free 3,000/mo (100/day); Postmark free 100/mo; SendGrid no longer free.
- **WhatsApp:** Meta test number → up to 5 recipients, free; production needs template approval and business verification; marketing messages ~$0.025 each in the US *unverified*.
