# Demo script: Ask-a-Trend (90 seconds)

Five beats, all on pages that are already loaded, so nothing waits on the AI or a crawl while the clock runs. About 220 spoken words. Built on the [PRD](prd-ask-a-trend-dashboard.md), Blair's [demo set](../data/curation/demo-set.md) and Yishan's demo pre-generator (`lib/engine/demo-plan.ts`).

**Site:** https://june-not-oct-hackathon.vercel.app

## Set up before you go on (tabs open left to right)

| Tab | Page | How to get there |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Damson Madder** brand page, scrolled to *Your style profile* | Home → Brands → Damson Madder |
| 2 | A **Damson Madder digest**, web view | Tab 1 → *Send test digest now* → *View it* (do this before you go on) |
| 3 | **Massimo Dutti** · *Which prints should we add for fall?* dashboard, fully loaded | Home → question box → brand Massimo Dutti → that example |

## The 90 seconds

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00–0:15** | (no click) | "Small fashion brands, the ones doing $10 to 100 million, do trend research with a founder and maybe one person. WGSN costs $10–35k a year, and no tool learns *their* style." |
| **0:15–0:30** | **Tab 1**: the profile | "Ask-a-Trend reads a brand's own website, just the words, and writes this style profile in under a minute. Romantic, playful prints, their price range. The founder can edit it." |
| **0:30–0:55** | **Tab 2**: the digest. Scroll one trend, click **👍 More like this** | "Then every week it sends trends that fit *that* brand, to email or Slack. Each one says why it's rising and why it fits, with real products on shelves now. Every thumbs up teaches it." |
| **0:55–1:15** | **Tab 3**: the dashboard. Click the top bar in **Rising patterns**; the evidence panel opens | "Or just ask. 'Which prints should we add for fall?' gets a dashboard built for that question, and every number opens the real products behind it." |
| **1:15–1:30** | Close the panel, then **Adjust what matters** → **Fashion house / editorial**; the bars reorder | "A mass-market brand and a fashion house care about different things, and it re-ranks instantly. A hundred to five hundred dollars a month. It lives in their inbox." |

**Cut if you're running long:** the *Adjust what matters* click (end at 1:15 with the price line).

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | Do this |
|---|---|
| Tab 3 is slow or errors | Talk over tab 2 and skip to the price line. |
| Digest tab didn't load | Show the *Your trend digest* list on tab 1 and say what it sends. |
| Site or network down | Play the screen recording of a rehearsal (record one when you rehearse). |
